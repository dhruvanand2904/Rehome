About ReHome

ReHome is a platform that enables people to buy and sell pre-loved home goods such as furniture, décor, and household items. Our mission is to make sustainable living simple by giving quality items a second life.

ReHome focuses exclusively on home goods, with an emphasis on quality, trust, and sustainability, rather than cluttered or unrelated listings.
